    Guidelines 21.9.26

    What gets checked

    Every message is checked against the guideline rules, then edited in the comms team's style. Each finding cites its guideline number.

    • Title and description50 characters, different, no “&”, year group first
    • Dates and times“Thursday, 6th November”, “7.45am”, weekday matches date, not in the past
    • House style“at” not “@”, single spaces, no caps, no emojis, AED3000, British English
    • School namesNo DOM/DAC, children vs students
    • LinksOn the word HERE, PDFs only, no QR codes
    • PersonalisationMerge fields instead of “your child”
    • FormattingMontserrat, black only, one font size, bold key details
    • Scheduling48 hours’ notice, no Fridays, archive the day after the event
    • Spelling, grammar and toneAI edit in the comms team’s style
