    What gets checked

    • Title and description – 50 characters, different, no “&”, year group first
    • Dates and times – “Thursday, 6th November”, “7.45am”, weekday matches date, not in the past
    • House style – “at” not “@”, single spaces, no caps, no emojis, AED3000, British English
    • School names – no DOM/DAC, children vs students
    • Links – on the word HERE, PDFs only, no QR codes
    • Personalisation – merge fields instead of “your child”
    • Formatting – Montserrat, black only, one font size, bold key details
    • Scheduling – 48 hours’ notice, no Fridays, archive the day after the event
    • Spelling, grammar and tone – AI edit in the comms team’s style
